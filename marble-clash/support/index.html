Support

Marble Clash is a 1v1 marbles duel: aim, pull the power bar and knock marbles out of the ring. Play against the computer on three difficulty levels, against a friend on the same phone, through 40 puzzle levels, or in the daily challenge. Win matches to unlock new marbles and tables, and collect stickers in the trophy room. The game is in English and Turkish.

Questions and problems

Found a bug, stuck on a puzzle or have an idea? Write to gokhanertogan@gmail.com. Please mention your phone model and the game version (shown on the App Store page or in your phone's settings) if you are reporting a problem.

Your progress

There is no account: your progress is saved on your phone only. Deleting the game deletes your progress, and it can't be restored from our side.

Privacy

The game collects no personal data. Read the privacy policy.

Destek

Marble Clash bire bir bir bilye düellosudur: nişan al, güç çubuğunu çek ve bilyeleri halkanın dışına at. Üç zorluk seviyesinde bilgisayara karşı, aynı telefonda bir arkadaşına karşı 40 bulmaca bölümünde ya da günlük görevde oyna. Maç kazandıkça yeni bilyeler ve masalar açılır, kupa odasında çıkartma toplarsın. Oyun Türkçe ve İngilizcedir.
