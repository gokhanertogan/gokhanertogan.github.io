Park Out – Support

Park Out is a parking-lot escape puzzle. The lot is packed with cars: tap a car and it drives the way its lights face. With a clear road it leaves the lot; with anything in the way it crashes. Find the right order and clear the lot. 160 levels in 16 chapters, endless play after them, a daily puzzle, and zen, speed run, weekly challenge and a level editor.

Questions and problems

Write to gokhanertogan@gmail.com. It helps to mention your phone model, its system version, the game's version (on the App Store page) and the level where the problem happened.

Good to know

  • No account is needed; your progress is saved only on your phone. Deleting the game deletes it.
  • The game has no ads and no purchases, and plays offline.
  • Settings (the gear on the level map) has sound, music, vibration, the daily reminder, language and the privacy policy.

Privacy

The game collects no personal data. Read the privacy policy.

Park Out – Destek

Park Out bir otopark bulmacasıdır. Otopark araçlarla dolu: bir araca dokun, farlarının baktığı yöne gitsin. Yol açıksa otoparktan çıkar; önünde bir şey varsa çarpar. Doğru sırayı bul ve otoparkı boşalt. 16 bölümde 160 seviye, ardından bitmeyen seviyeler, her gün yeni bir bulmaca; ayrıca Zen, hız koşusu, haftalık meydan okuma ve seviye editörü.
