Park Out – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026 · Türkçe

Park Out is a puzzle game made by Gökhan Ertoğan ("we"). In short: the game collects no data about you. There are no ads, no accounts, no purchases and no analytics, and we run no servers that the game talks to.

What stays on your phone

The game saves its own data only on your device: your progress and stars, coins, hints, daily puzzle streak, car styles and settings (sound, vibration, language). It never leaves your phone and we never receive it. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

What we don't do

No ads, no tracking, no advertising identifier, no accounts, no chat, no content shared by players, no location, no contacts, no camera or microphone. Nothing is sold or shared.

Store ratings

The game may ask once whether you'd like to rate it. That dialog belongs to Google Play or the App Store; the game learns nothing about what you do with it.

Children

Park Out is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If a later version changes any of this (for example, by adding ads), this policy will be updated with a new date before that version is released, and the game will ask what it needs to ask at that point.
