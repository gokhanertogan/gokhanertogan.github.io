Park Out – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026 · Türkçe

Park Out is a puzzle game made by Gökhan Ertoğan ("we"). In short: the game collects no data about you. There are no ads, no accounts, no purchases and no analytics, and we run no servers of our own. On iPhone the game also checks at launch whether an update is required (see "Update check on iPhone").

What stays on your phone

The game saves its own data only on your device: your progress and stars, coins, hints, daily puzzle streak and streak freezes, achievements, records from the other modes, your city and the weekly event, car styles, trails and horns, and settings (sound, music, vibration, language, reminder). It never leaves your phone and we never receive it. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Update check on iPhone

On iPhone, the game downloads a small settings file from gokhanertogan.github.io (hosted by GitHub) when it starts, to check whether this version still works or an update is required. The request carries no information about you or your device: no identifiers, no progress, nothing about how you play. Like any website, GitHub sees the IP address the request comes from; we don't receive it, and nothing is stored. Without an internet connection the game simply skips the check and plays as usual.

Daily reminder

If you turn it on (it starts off), the game asks your phone for permission to show notifications and then sets a reminder for the evening on days the daily puzzle isn't solved yet. The reminder is made and shown entirely by your phone: nothing is sent anywhere. You can turn it off in Settings, or in your phone's settings.

Sharing

After solving the daily puzzle you can tap Share (behind a question for grown-ups). The game then hands your phone's own share menu a short text and a picture of the same: the puzzle number, your stars, a row of colored squares for your taps, your tap count and streak. In the level editor you can share a level the same way, as a code that holds only the layout you made. You choose where it goes, or cancel. The game itself sends nothing anywhere and learns nothing about what you do with it. A code someone gives you is typed or pasted in by you and stays on your phone.

What we don't do

No ads, no tracking, no advertising identifier, no accounts, no chat, no player content inside the game, no location, no contacts, no camera or microphone. Nothing is sold or shared.

Store ratings

The game may ask once whether you'd like to rate it. That dialog belongs to Google Play or the App Store; the game learns nothing about what you do with it.

Children

Park Out is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If a later version changes any of this (for example, by adding ads), this policy will be updated with a new date before that version is released, and the game will ask what it needs to ask at that point.
