Support

Snowball Duel is a winter game made by Gökhan Ertoğan. Hold to roll a snowball, slide to aim, let go to throw, and hide behind your snow wall. Beat the rivals of the Winter Village, the Champion Tour and the North Tour, climb the Snow League, race down five sled hills, and dress up your kid, pet and sled. Play against the phone or a friend on the same phone. No account is needed and there are no ads or purchases.

Questions and problems

Found a bug, stuck somewhere or have an idea? Write to gokhanertogan@gmail.com. It helps to mention your phone model, its system version and the game's version (shown in your phone's settings or the store page).

Your progress

Your progress is saved on your phone only. Deleting the game deletes it, and it cannot be restored from our side, because we never receive it.

Privacy

The game collects no personal data. Read the full privacy policy.

Destek

Snowball Duel, Gökhan Ertoğan tarafından geliştirilen bir kış oyunudur. Basılı tutup kartopu yuvarla, kaydırıp nişan al, bırakıp at ve kar duvarının arkasına saklan. Kış Köyü'nün, Şampiyon Turu'nun ve Kuzey Turu'nun rakiplerini yen, Kar Ligi'nde yüksel, beş kızak pistinden aşağı kay; çocuğunu, evcil hayvanını ve kızağını giydir. Telefona karşı ya da aynı telefonda bir arkadaşınla oyna. Hesap gerekmez; reklam ve satın alma yoktur.
