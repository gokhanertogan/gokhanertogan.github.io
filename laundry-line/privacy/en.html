Laundry Line – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026 · Türkçe

Laundry Line is a puzzle game made by Gökhan Ertoğan ("we"). In short: the game collects no data about you. There are no ads, no accounts, no purchases and no analytics, it needs no internet connection, and we run no servers that the game talks to.

What stays on your phone

The game saves its own data only on your device: your stars, the socks in your album, an unfinished level so you can carry on later, and your settings (language, sound, music, vibration, reduced motion, cat visits). It never leaves your phone and we never receive it. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

What we don't do

No ads, no tracking, no advertising identifier, no accounts, no chat, no content shared by players, no location, no contacts, no camera or microphone. The game asks for no permissions. Nothing is sold or shared.

Children

Laundry Line is made for children aged 5 to 12 and for everyone else. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If a later version changes any of this (for example, by adding ads), this policy will be updated with a new date before that version is released, and the game will ask what it needs to ask at that point.
