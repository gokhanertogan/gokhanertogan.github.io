Dog Walk – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026 · Türkçe

Dog Walk is a game for children made by Gökhan Ertoğan ("we"). In short: the game collects no data about you or your child. There are no ads, no accounts, no purchases and no analytics, it needs no internet connection, and we run no servers that the game talks to.

What stays on your phone

The game saves its own data only on your device: the dogs in your family and their outfits, stars and bones, photos taken at photo stops, keepsakes, badges, and your settings (language, sound, music, poop pickup). It never leaves your phone and we never receive it. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

What we don't do

No ads, no tracking, no advertising identifier, no accounts, no chat, no content shared by players, no location, no contacts, no camera or microphone. The "photos" in the game are drawings the game makes itself; it never uses the phone's camera or photo library. The game asks for no permissions. Nothing is sold or shared.

Children

Dog Walk is made for children aged 5 to 12. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included. The grown-ups corner in Settings sits behind a press-and-hold gate.

Changes

If a later version changes any of this (for example, by adding optional purchases), this policy will be updated with a new date before that version is released, and the game will ask what it needs to ask at that point.
