About the game

Pillow Sumo is a one-button pillow fight on a bed, made by Gökhan Ertoğan. Tap to swing, hold and release for a heavy hit, swing back at the last moment to parry, and push your rival off the bed. Follow the hunt for the Golden Pillow through two chapters and nine rooms, beat each rival's signature move and unlock them as playable characters. No accounts, no ads, no purchases; your progress stays on your device.

Help

How do I play? The first time you press Play, a short training teaches each control. You can replay it from the home screen.

The game asks me to update. Open the App Store, update Pillow Sumo, and keep playing; your progress is kept.

My progress is gone. Progress is saved only on your device, so deleting the game deletes it. A device backup restores it.
